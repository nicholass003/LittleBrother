## Versioning

LittleBrother follows a **pre-release versioning model** during early development:

* **ALPHA** — Early experimental development builds
* **BETA** — Feature-complete but still under testing
* **RC** — Release candidates before stable releases
* **Stable** — Production-ready releases

---

## Notes

⚠ Early releases may contain breaking changes as the architecture evolves.
